Two homepage heroes are compared. Both use the headline GRAND AMOUR. PETITS PRIX.
A. Studio Pop: happy golden retriever and tabby cat cut out on a bold purple panel with an orange blob. Most pages use pets on flat purple and orange studio backgrounds. Sells dogs and cats equally. Very branded and clean.
B. Sunny Home: a smiling young Moroccan woman in a cream hijab hugging a tabby cat, in a warm Moroccan home with zellige and a lantern. Very emotional and local, but the photos would be AI people, cats lead here, and dog owners are not shown in the hero, and home scenes are harder to keep consistent across 30 images.
C: A merge: Studio Pop for the site system and hero, with the Sunny Home photo used as the big brand story section further down the homepage.
